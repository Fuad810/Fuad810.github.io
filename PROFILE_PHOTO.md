# Profile photo

The portfolio is ready for your picture.

## To add your photo
1. Choose a professional headshot (square or portrait works best).
2. Rename it exactly to `profile.jpg`.
3. Upload `profile.jpg` to the **root** of this repository, next to `index.html`.
4. The portfolio can then be updated to display it in the profile area.

Recommended: clear face, simple background, good lighting, and no large text/watermarks.
